Our Equipment

Technology That Supports Every Step

Diagnosis & Imaging

Detailed tests that show what is happening inside the eye.

OCT Scanner

Cross-sectional scans of the retina and optic nerve.

Used for

  • Retina Clinic
  • Glaucoma Clinic
  • Diabetic Eye Screening

Retinal Camera

Wide photos of the back of the eye to record and compare changes.

Used for

  • Diabetic Eye Screening
  • Retinal Holes & Diabetic Retinopathy

Slit Lamp Microscope

Magnified examination of the front and inside of the eye.

Used for

  • Comprehensive Eye Check-up
  • Eye Injury Treatment
  • Meibomian Gland Probing

Visual Field Analyser

Maps your side vision to detect and track glaucoma damage.

Used for

  • Glaucoma Clinic

Tonometer

Measures the pressure inside the eye in seconds.

Used for

  • Glaucoma Clinic
  • Comprehensive Eye Check-up

Corneal Topography

Maps the shape of the cornea for lens fitting and keratoconus care.

Used for

  • Contact Lens & Keratoconus

Auto-refractor & Phoropter

Accurate measurement of your glasses prescription.

Used for

  • Comprehensive Eye Check-up
  • Paediatric Ophthalmology
  • Low Vision Aids

Surgery

Precise tools for safe, accurate eye surgery.

Optical Biometer

Measures the eye to calculate the right lens power for cataract surgery.

Used for

  • Cataract Surgery

Phaco System & Surgical Microscope

Micro-incision cataract surgery under high magnification.

Used for

  • Cataract Surgery
  • Pterygium Excision
  • Eye Injury Treatment

Laser Treatment

Quick clinic procedures without any incision.

YAG Laser

Clears clouding after cataract surgery and treats some types of glaucoma.

Used for

  • Glaucoma & After-Cataract Care

Green Laser

Seals retinal tears and treats diabetic changes in the retina.

Used for

  • Retinal Holes & Diabetic Retinopathy
  • Retina Clinic
