SECTION 3: OUR EYE CARE SERVICES

Our Eye Care Services

Specialist eye care in Barka, from routine eye examinations to advanced treatment options.

Cataract Clinic

Cataracts can cause cloudy vision, glare and difficulty seeing clearly. Our clinic provides cataract assessments and discusses suitable treatment options, including surgery when indicated.

CTA: Learn More

Glaucoma Clinic

Glaucoma can damage the optic nerve and affect vision over time. Our services include assessment, monitoring and management options tailored to individual needs.

CTA: Learn More

Retina Clinic

The retina plays an essential role in vision. Our retina services support the assessment and management of retinal conditions, including diabetic retinopathy.

CTA: Learn More

Paediatric Ophthalmology

Children may experience vision problems that are difficult to recognise. Our paediatric eye-care services help assess and manage eye conditions affecting children.

CTA: Learn More

Keratoconus Clinic

Keratoconus affects the shape of the cornea and may cause blurred or distorted vision. Our clinic provides assessment and discusses appropriate management options.

CTA: Learn More

Oculoplastic Clinic

Oculoplastic care addresses selected conditions affecting the eyelids and surrounding structures. Our team assesses these concerns and discusses appropriate treatment options.

CTA: Learn More

Pterygium Excision

Pterygium is a growth of tissue on the surface of the eye that may cause irritation and affect vision. Our eye-care team assesses the condition and discusses suitable treatment options, including surgical removal when indicated.

CTA: Learn More

Pleoptic and Orthoptic Treatment of Strabismus & Amblyopia

Strabismus (squint) and amblyopia (lazy eye) can affect eye alignment and visual development. Our services include assessment and appropriate pleoptic and orthoptic treatment options based on individual needs.

SECTION 6: ADVANCED TECHNOLOGY

Advanced Technology for Better Eye Care

Diagnostic and treatment technology helps our ophthalmologists assess eye conditions and plan appropriate care.

Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT)

Provides detailed cross-sectional images of structures such as the retina and optic nerve.

Optical Biometry

Laser Technology

Diagnostic Imaging

SECTION 8: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What services does Majan Eye Centre provide?

Majan Eye Centre offers a range of eye-care services, including cataract, glaucoma, retina, paediatric ophthalmology, oculoplastic care, keratoconus assessment, LASIK and general eye examinations.

2. How can I book an appointment at Majan Eye Centre?

You can request an appointment by calling +968 9177 4829. Contact our team to enquire about appointment availability and the service you require.

3. Do I need a referral to see an ophthalmologist?

Contact our team to confirm the appointment requirements for your preferred service and whether any referral or previous medical records are needed.

4. Does Majan Eye Centre perform LASIK?

Yes, Majan Eye Centre confirms that it offers LASIK. An eye examination is necessary to determine whether laser vision correction is suitable for you.

5. Does Majan Eye Centre provide cataract surgery?

Majan Eye Centre provides cataract services. Contact the clinic to confirm the available surgical services and arrange a consultation to discuss your needs.

6. Where is Majan Eye Centre located?

Majan Eye Centre is located in Barka, Sultanate of Oman. Visit our contact page for location details and directions.

7. What should I bring to my eye appointment?

Bring any previous eye examination reports, your current glasses or contact lens details, relevant medical records and a list of medications, if applicable.
