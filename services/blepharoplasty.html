What is Blepharoplasty?

Blepharoplasty is surgery to remove or reshape excess skin, muscle and fat from the upper or lower eyelids.

With age, eyelid skin stretches and the fat around the eye can bulge. Heavy upper lids can block your upper field of vision, and lower lid bags can make you look tired.

The surgery can improve vision when the lids hang over the eyes, and it can also refresh the appearance of the eyes.

At a glance

  • Also called Eyelid lift
  • Who it suits Mostly adults over 35
  • Purpose Vision and appearance
  • Anaesthesia Usually local
02

Signs You May Benefit

You may benefit from blepharoplasty if you have:

  • Lids blocking your view

    Excess upper lid skin limits your upper vision.

  • Tired appearance

    You look tired even when you are rested.

  • Bags under the eyes

    Puffy lower lids that do not go away.

  • Brow strain

    Raising your eyebrows to see better.

  • Skin irritation

    Skin folds rubbing on the lashes.

  • Difficulty with glasses

    Heavy lids touch the lenses.

03

When is Surgery Recommended?

Surgery is recommended when excess skin affects your vision or comfort, or when you would like to improve the appearance of your eyelids and are in good general health.

Realistic expectations matter. We will show you what the surgery can and cannot change before you decide.

Blepharoplasty may be right for you if

  • Upper lid skin hangs over your lashes
  • You have persistent bags under the eyes
  • Your eyelids make you look tired
  • You are in good general health
  • Your eye health has been checked

05

Procedure

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We discuss your goals and examine your eyelids and eyes.

  2. 2

    Marking

    The exact amount of skin to remove is marked on the lids.

  3. 3

    Surgery

    Excess skin and fat are removed through incisions in natural creases.

  4. 4

    Going home

    You go home the same day with ointment and cold compresses.

06

Recovery

  1. First 2–3 days

    Swelling and bruising peak. Use cold compresses and rest.

  2. About 1 week

    Stitches are removed. You can usually go out with sunglasses.

  3. 2–3 weeks

    Most bruising has faded and you can return to work.

  4. 3–6 months

    Scars fade and the final result settles.

Do

  • Sleep with your head raised
  • Use cold compresses for 48 hours
  • Apply ointment as advised
  • Wear sunglasses outdoors

Avoid

  • Heavy exercise for 2 weeks
  • Eye make-up for 2 weeks
  • Rubbing the eyelids
  • Blood-thinning medicines unless approved
