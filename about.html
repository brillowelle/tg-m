About Majan Eye Centre

Majan Eye Centre is an eye-care centre located in Barka, Oman, providing patients with access to a range of ophthalmic services. Our aim is to help patients understand their eye health and explore appropriate care for their vision needs.

Whether you need a routine eye examination or an assessment for a specific eye condition, our team can help you enquire about available services and the next steps for your care.

Our Approach

Our Approach to Eye Care

We aim to help patients make informed decisions about their eye health through accessible information and appropriate clinical assessment. Our approach focuses on:

  • Patient-focused care

    Understanding patients’ concerns and individual eye-care needs.

  • Eye health assessment

    Helping patients enquire about examinations and assessments appropriate to their symptoms.

  • Condition-specific care

    Providing information about different eye conditions and their management.

  • Ongoing guidance

    Encouraging patients to follow clinical advice and attend recommended follow-up appointments.

Specific treatments and procedures depend on the patient’s diagnosis, clinical assessment and the services available at the centre.
