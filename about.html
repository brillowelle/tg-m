About Us

Your Better Vision Is Our Lifelong Commitment

At Majan Eye Care, we are dedicated to providing exceptional eye care services with a focus on precision, comfort, and innovation at the heart of Barka . Our team of experienced professionals offers personalized eye care solutions, from routine checkups to advanced treatments. Trust us to safeguard your eye health and enhance your quality of life.

Mission

At Majan Eye Centre, we provide personalized, comprehensive eye care through advanced diagnostics, skilled professionals, and evidence-based practices, with a focus on patient satisfaction and safety.

Vision

To be a trusted leader in ophthalmic care across Oman, offering world-class eye treatments with integrity, innovation, and empathy. We'll ensure that every patient experiences the highest level of care.

Values

We prioritize trust, empathy, and human connection. Every patient is treated with respect and care, like a family member, offering not just solutions, but peace of mind and a future seen clearly.

Why Choose Us

Trusted by Thousands, Driven by Care

At Majan Eye Centre, we’re not just an eye hospital we’re a vision partner for life. From restoring clarity through cataract surgery to protecting sight with expert glaucoma care, from nurturing young vision in our paediatric clinic to managing delicate conditions like diabetic retinopathy, we offer more than services we offer peace of mind. With YAG laser therapy and Intravitreal injections, we bring international standards to your doorstep in Barka. For every eye, every age, and every need, we see you and we care you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Majan Eye Centre offers comprehensive eye care through its Cataract, Glaucoma, Retina, Paediatric Ophthalmology, Oculoplastic and Contact Lens/Keratoconus clinics, along with comprehensive eye check-ups, diabetic eye screening and a range of minor eye procedures. You can see the full list on our Services page.

You can request an appointment through our online booking form, message us on WhatsApp, or call us on +968 9177 4829.

You can book a consultation with our ophthalmologists directly, using the booking form, WhatsApp or phone. If you have health insurance, please check with your provider whether they need a referral or prior approval before your visit.

Please contact us to confirm whether LASIK is currently available at the centre. Our ophthalmologists can examine your eyes and discuss the vision-correction options suitable for you, including glasses and contact lenses.

Yes. Our Cataract Clinic assesses and treats cataracts, including phaco cataract surgery, in which the cloudy natural lens is replaced with a clear artificial lens. Your ophthalmologist will examine your eyes and explain the options that suit you.

Majan Eye Centre is in Barka, Sultanate of Oman, near Shams Al Khaleej Markets. Open our location in Google Maps.
