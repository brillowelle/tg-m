Insurance

Health Insurance at Majan Eye Centre

Health insurance coverage depends on your insurance provider, policy terms and the specific eye-care service you need.

If you plan to use health insurance for an examination, consultation or procedure, contact Majan Eye Centre before your appointment to confirm whether your policy is accepted and what requirements apply.

Insurance acceptance does not necessarily mean that every service or treatment is covered.

Your Coverage

Check Your Insurance Coverage

Your insurer can provide details about your policy benefits and coverage conditions.

Before visiting the clinic, we recommend checking the following details:

  • Whether your insurance provider is accepted by the clinic.
  • Whether your policy covers the consultation or treatment you require.
  • Whether a referral or prior approval is necessary.
  • Whether co-payments, deductibles or other charges apply.
  • Whether any exclusions or coverage limits affect your treatment.

Payment

Payment Information

If you are paying directly for your eye-care visit, contact Majan Eye Centre to enquire about consultation fees, procedure costs and accepted payment methods.

The total cost may depend on the type of examination, diagnostic tests, treatment recommended and individual clinical requirements.

For accurate pricing, ask the clinic for the current fees relevant to your appointment.

Your Visit

What to Bring to Your Appointment

To help the clinic process your visit, consider bringing:

  • A valid identification document.
  • Your health insurance card or policy information, if applicable.
  • Any referral or pre-authorisation documents required by your insurer.
  • Previous eye examination reports or relevant medical records.
  • Your current glasses or contact lens prescription, if available.
  • A list of medications you currently use.

Contact the clinic beforehand if you are unsure which documents are required.
