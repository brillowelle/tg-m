How It Works

Using Your Insurance

  1. 1

    Check your policy

    See what your plan covers for eye care, or ask your insurer.

  2. 2

    Book your visit

    Mention your insurer when you book so we can prepare.

  3. 3

    Bring your documents

  4. 4

    We handle the claim

    We confirm cover, and you pay any co-payment or uncovered amount.

  • Pay any co-payment by cash or card
